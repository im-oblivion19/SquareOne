About SquareOne

This idea started way back in 2018 during an arts class, where Mugdha was doing homework during arts class, and realised that the concept of parallel lines and angles applies heavily to the little paper frog sitting on the desk. Together with Karen, Harold, Chad, and of course Abby, Mugdha decided to build a comprehensive project where she tries to present how Math pretty much applies in every aspect of this world, including this little hobby that kids learn in crafts class. SquareOne explores the connection between origami, mathematics, geography, and design. The project uses an interactive world map to show where major origami-related mathematical ideas came from and how each one connects to folding, structure, and geometry.

Website Timeline

2018

Concept

The project began as an idea to connect origami with mathematical discovery across different parts of the world.

2022

Research

The team selected key theorems and mathematical ideas, including Kawasaki's theorem, Beloch's fold, Lang's tree theorem, the fold-and-cut theorem, and Gaussian curvature.

2024

Map Design

The interactive map was built with country pins, hover boxes, and short descriptions for each theorem location.

2026

Final Website

The final version added styled information cards, a cleaner layout, and an about page explaining the team and purpose of the project.
